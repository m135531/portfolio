Away From The Screen ✦

Things I enjoy when I'm not coding.

Technology takes up a large part of my world, but some of my favourite activities happen away from an IDE. These interests help me balance analytical thinking with creativity and curiosity.

Reading

I enjoy fiction and nonfiction, including The Shining and 1984. I also like books about number theory because they connect stories, ideas and mathematical curiosity.

Walking & Exploring

In my free time, I enjoy walking and exploring new places. These small journeys help me notice new surroundings, recharge and return to my work with a fresh perspective.

Problem Solving

I enjoy challenging mathematics problems because they reward creativity, pattern recognition and persistence. Finding an elegant solution is one of my favourite ways to think deeply.

“The most interesting problems are usually the ones that need both structure and imagination.”
